# Product Manual: Nebula-X9 Smart Toaster
**Version:** 2.4 | **Release Date:** October 24, 2025
**Manufacturer:** Orion Kitchen Dynamics

## 1. Product Overview
The Nebula-X9 is not just a toaster; it is a quantum-heated bread optimization unit. Unlike traditional toasters that use nichrome wires, the Nebula-X9 utilizes "Photon-Stream Technology" to brown bread evenly at the molecular level.

## 2. Technical Specifications
* **Power Output:** 1800 Watts (Standard Mode), 3200 Watts (Hyper-Crunch Mode).
* **Dimensions:** 30cm x 15cm x 20cm.
* **Weight:** 4.2 kg (due to the internal lead shielding).
* **Connectivity:** Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.3, and Zigbee.
* **Max Temperature:** 450°C (Safety cutoff at 460°C).

## 3. Unique Features
### 3.1. The "Soggy-Bottom" Prevention System
The device includes three humidity sensors located at the base of the slots. If moisture is detected exceeding 14%, the active ventilation fans engage at 4000 RPM to pre-dry the slice before toasting begins.

### 3.2. Voice Commands
The Nebula-X9 listens for the wake word "Hey Crumb-Master."
* "Hey Crumb-Master, light toast" -> Sets intensity to level 2.
* "Hey Crumb-Master, charcoal mode" -> Sets intensity to level 10 (Warning: Fire hazard).

## 4. Troubleshooting and Error Codes
* **Error E-01:** Bread stuck. Do not use a fork. Engage the "Eject-O-Matic" lever on the left side.
* **Error E-99:** Quantum destabilization detected. Unplug immediately and run away.
* **Error Wi-Fi:** If the toaster cannot connect to the internet, it will refuse to toast bagels as a security measure (DRM protection).

## 5. Warranty Information
The warranty covers the heating element for 5 years. However, the warranty is void if the user attempts to toast "Pop-Tarts" without the authorized foil wrapper sleeve (sold separately).
